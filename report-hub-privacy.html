Privacy Policy — Stegos Report Hub

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Operated by Stegos Global ("Stegos Global", "we") · Contact: nishant@stegosglobal.com

What this service does

Stegos Report Hub is a reporting tool for Amazon sellers and advertisers. It shows sales, advertising and category reports for accounts we manage on our clients' behalf.

What we collect

  • Account data: name and email of users who log in.
  • Amazon data: advertising and sales reports for the accounts a client authorises us to manage, supplied by uploaded files or by the Amazon Ads API and Selling Partner API.
  • Usage logs: logins, uploads and changes, for security.

We do not collect payment card details, and we do not sell personal data.

How we use it

Only to provide reports and analysis to the client who owns the Amazon account, to secure the service, and to meet legal obligations. A client sees only their own account's data.

Who we share it with

Only our infrastructure providers (database hosting and web hosting), under their data-protection terms and only to run the service. We do not share Amazon data with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role, and credentials are stored securely. We keep data while the client uses the service and delete it within 30 days of a written request or the end of the relationship, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by writing to nishant@stegosglobal.com.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.
