Straight answers

Questions sellers ask first.

Short, plain answers. If yours isn't here, ask — we reply with questions about your account, not a sales script.

Which marketplaces do you work on?

Amazon (India and international storefronts, including Amazon Global Selling account support), Flipkart and Myntra. Flipkart and Myntra are India-only marketplaces. We work on marketplaces only — no broad performance-marketing menu.

What happens in the free ad audit?

We map spend, structure, catalogue health and where efficiency is leaking, then use the call to identify wasted spend, missing structure and your next clean test.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in. We earn the next month with clear actions and visible progress — you stay because the work is useful, not because a contract is hard to exit.

How will I see what's happening in my account?

Conversations start with the account, not a polished monthly deck. You see the levers behind spend, ACOS and ROAS, and we review what worked and where the next rupee should go every week.

Do you also handle cataloging?

Yes, as an add-on across all marketplaces: listing creation and optimisation, A+ content coordination, catalog health audits, and variation and compliance fixes.

Why don't you show client brand names?

Results are shown by product category rather than brand name to protect client privacy. We also include accounts where efficiency softened or ACOS rose — not just the wins. See the case studies.
